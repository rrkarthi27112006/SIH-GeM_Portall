FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
4
@BN - 
BidNova
FEASIBILITY:
Technical:
 Uses proven technologies — React, 
FastAPI
, PostgreSQL, OCR, LLM, RAG & ML.
AI:
 Reads tender documents, extracts requirements and matches them with submitted documents.
Integration:
 Uses authorised government APIs where available; mock APIs for the prototype.
Security:
 JWT, role-based access, encryption and audit logs protect procurement data.
Human Oversight:
 AI assists with verification; 
final decisions stay with the Procurement Officer.
VIABILITY:
Operational:
 Reduces manual document checking and saves time.
Economic:
 Cuts repetitive work and improves evaluation efficiency.
Scalable:
 Can support new departments, tender types and compliance checks.
Standardised:
 Provides one consistent verification process.
Auditable:
 Stores evidence, sources and verification history.
TECHNICAL CHALLENGES & MITIGATION:
1.Government API Availability
Risk :
 Some government sources may not provide a usable public API.
Mitigation :
 Use official/authorised integrations where possible and 
mock APIs during the prototype
.
2.Poor-Quality Documents
Risk :
 Blurry or scanned documents can cause OCR mistakes.
Mitigation :
 Use 
image preprocessing, OCR confidence checks and human verification
.
3.Company Name Confusion
Risk :
 Two companies may have similar names, which can lead to incorrect matching.
Mitigation :
 Verify using 
GSTIN, PAN, CIN and other unique identifiers
.
4.Changing Tender Requirements
Risk :
 Requirements can differ from one tender to another.
Mitigation :
 Extract requirements dynamically and keep the 
compliance rules configurable
.


=== SLIDE 5 ===
IMPACT AND BENEFITS
Procurement Efficiency:
 Automated verification of bidder documents and government records can significantly reduce repetitive manual verification effort and speed up tender evaluation.
• Improved Compliance & Accuracy:
 AI-based extraction and cross-verification help identify missing documents, data mismatches and potential compliance issues, reducing human errors and inconsistencies.
• Transparency & Governance:
 Evidence-based verification, compliance scores and complete audit trails provide procurement officers with a transparent and traceable basis for review.
• Better Risk Identification:
 Early detection of discrepancies, missing requirements and potential blacklisting/debarment issues enables faster and more informed scrutiny of bidders.
